# Acme Product FAQ

## AcmePhone X

**What is the battery life of the AcmePhone X?**
The AcmePhone X lasts up to 22 hours of video playback on a single charge. Standby time is approximately 14 days.

**Is the AcmePhone X waterproof?**
The AcmePhone X carries an IP68 rating. It withstands submersion up to 6 meters for 30 minutes. Water damage is not covered under the standard warranty.

**What is the warranty period for the AcmePhone X?**
The AcmePhone X includes a 12-month limited manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

**How do I reset my AcmePhone X?**
Hold the power and volume-down buttons together for 10 seconds until the Acme logo appears. This performs a soft reset and does not erase data.

## AcmeBook Pro (Laptop)

**What ports does the AcmeBook Pro have?**
The AcmeBook Pro has two USB-C / Thunderbolt 4 ports, one HDMI 2.1 port, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and an SD card reader.

**Can I upgrade the RAM on the AcmeBook Pro?**
No. The RAM on the AcmeBook Pro is soldered to the mainboard and cannot be upgraded after purchase. Choose your configuration at checkout.

**What is the return window for the AcmeBook Pro?**
AcmeBook Pro can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it is in original condition with all accessories.

## AcmeBuds (Wireless Earbuds)

**Do AcmeBuds support noise cancellation?**
Yes. AcmeBuds feature Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) with a Transparency mode for hearing your surroundings.

**How long do AcmeBuds take to charge?**
A full charge takes about 60 minutes. A 10-minute quick charge provides roughly 2 hours of listening time.

**What is the warranty period for AcmeBuds?**
AcmeBuds include a 12-month limited warranty. Lost earbuds are not covered, but replacements can be purchased individually.
